Who we are

MAD WORLD is a hopping arcade game made by SYHU Studio and published by SYHU Inc (“we”). This policy explains what the game does with information when you play it on Android or on the web.

What the game stores

To remember your game between sessions, MAD WORLD saves a few values in the app's own storage on your device:

  • your Root Coins, best score and the characters you have unlocked or picked;
  • your settings, such as sound on or off and the graphics quality;
  • when you last opened the free gift, so the next one can count down, and that you have had the welcome gift.

These values never leave your device. We cannot see them, and they are not linked to your name, email or any account. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not show ads or sell anything in this version.
  • It does not send your game data to us or to anyone else.

The Android app has the standard internet permission that its game engine includes, but the game itself runs entirely from files inside the app.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Future changes

A later version may add rewarded ads (through Google AdMob) or in-app purchases (through Google Play). If it does, those services handle their own data under Google's policies, and we will update this page and its date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SYHU Inc through the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
